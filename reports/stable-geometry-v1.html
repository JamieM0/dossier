Frozen design and limits
  • All three MovieLens cohorts are prior evaluation cohorts, not untouched confirmation data.
  • The experiment measures ranking over fixed held-out films, not candidate retrieval.
  • The candidate receives ratings and descriptive TMDB metadata only.

The same candidate catalogue and hidden ratings score every strategy. Only supplied ratings reach the CLI. The test changes grouping, claim reconciliation, and resulting scores together because those parts form one taste representation.

The protocol records fixture hashes, evaluator hashes, thresholds, checkpoints, and decision gates. A changed fixture requires a new protocol version.
